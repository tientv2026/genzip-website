GenZip is a free archive manager for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). The first build, **0.9.0-beta.1**, is in testing and will be released officially through the **Microsoft Store**.

## Why another archiver?

Most of us only need a few things: open a downloaded archive, pull out a few files, zip a folder to send it. GenZip is built for exactly that, with a compact Windows-style interface, Vietnamese and English, and light or dark themes.

GenZip does not reinvent compression. All compression and extraction is done by the **official 7-Zip 26.05 engine**, so speed and compatibility are on par with 7-Zip. GenZip takes care of the interface, safety and everyday conveniences. Real measurements are on the [Speed](/en/benchmark) page.

## What's in 0.9

- **Opens almost any archive:** ZIP, 7Z, RAR/RAR5, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ZST, ISO, CAB, WIM, split `.001` files.
- **Creates ZIP, 7Z and TAR** with built-in profiles (General, Maximum compression, Fast, Store), split volumes, AES-256 encryption and self-extracting `.exe` from 7Z.
- **Preview** images and text inside an archive (F3) without extracting.
- **Edit open archives** (ZIP, 7Z, TAR): add, delete, rename, drag files into the window.
- **Real pause**, run in background, and a **task queue** when you start several jobs in a row.
- **Integrity test** with an exportable `.txt` report.
- **Windows 11 right-click menu:** extract here, extract to a folder of the same name, quick ZIP/7Z.

## Private by default

No ads, no account, no tracking. The Microsoft Store build never connects to the network by itself; the Store handles updates.

## When can I get it?

GenZip is awaiting release on the Microsoft Store. Once the official link is live, the button on the [Download](/en/download) page will take you straight to the Store. There is no free-floating GenZip installer, so be careful with sites claiming to offer one.

Meanwhile, see [all features](/en/features) or read the [user guide](/en/guide).
